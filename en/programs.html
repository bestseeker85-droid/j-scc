Programs

Programs

Student programs connect what is learned in class to industry experience and jobs; a second set is for working engineers and companies who join us in Jeju.

For students

Industry lectures & mentoring

Engineers from partner companies talk about current technology and their day-to-day work.

Site visits & internships

Visits and hands-on practice at research institutes and companies.

Showcase & job matching

Student presentations and awards, meetings and hiring with partner companies.

For working engineers and companies

Courses for working engineers

Short hands-on courses such as FPGA and RF.

Workation & labcation

Companies and engineers from outside Jeju stay here for courses and joint work.

Joint workshops

Workshops with partner companies on technology exchange and hiring.
