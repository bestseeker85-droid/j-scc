Greeting

Thank you for visiting the J-SCC website.

Semiconductors account for more than half of Jeju's exports, but until now there have been few opportunities in Jeju to learn chip design, verification and measurement and to build a career on them.

J-SCC was set up to create that path, by Jeju National University together with Jeju Special Self-Governing Province, local companies and research institutes. We add practical courses to the regular curriculum so that participants can try everything themselves, from simulation to chip measurement. The courses are open not only to electronics majors but also to students from other schools in Jeju, working engineers and anyone who wants to understand semiconductors first-hand.

We hope the people who grow here will go on to do good work in Jeju and wherever they choose. We look forward to your interest and participation.

Principal Investigator, J-SCC
