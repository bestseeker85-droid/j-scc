Greeting

Thank you for visiting J-SCC, the Jeju Silicon Convergence Center.

Semiconductors account for more than half of Jeju's exports, yet the path to learning and working in chip design, verification and measurement here in Jeju has been narrow.

J-SCC is a semiconductor talent platform led by Jeju National University and built with the province, local companies and research institutes. We add hands-on courses on top of the regular curriculum, let students experience everything from simulation to chip measurement, and connect that experience to jobs and life in Jeju.

Director, J-SCC
