J-SCC · Jeju Silicon Convergence Center

Learn in Jeju.

J-SCC is a semiconductor talent program led by Jeju National University together with Jeju Special Self-Governing Province, Jeju Technopark, local companies and research institutes. Students learn the practical work from design through chip measurement, and go on to careers in Jeju and around the world.

Who we train

Who we train, and where they fit in the full cycle

The curriculum is organized around the eight core fab processes, with the design work that comes before them and the testing that follows: ① device design and process, ② circuit design and fabrication, ③ system design, ④ test and measurement. Using campus labs together with partner facilities, students go through the whole cycle at least once, fabricate a real chip and build a module around it. The goal is not production-line staff but practical engineers who understand the full cycle and can work in Jeju or anywhere else.

① Device Design & Process (partial)Transistor design (TCAD) · device characteristics · hands-on with part of the front-end (oxidation, photo, etch, deposition)
② Circuit Design & FabricationAnalog, mixed-signal and RF circuits — simulation, layout, tape-out
③ System DesignDigital and systems — Verilog, FPGA, RISC-V, SoC
④ Test & MeasurementBack-end — EDS test, packaging; device, circuit and module measurement

Chips designed here are fabricated at an external foundry through an MPW run. Measuring the returned chip and building a module with it completes the cycle.

The four fields are the eight core processes (wafer fabrication, oxidation, photolithography, etch, deposition/ion implantation, metallization, EDS test, packaging) plus the design before them and the verification after.
