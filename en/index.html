J-SCC · Jeju Silicon Convergence Center

Learn in Jeju.
Work in Jeju.
Thrive worldwide.

J-SCC is a semiconductor talent program led by Jeju National University together with Jeju Special Self-Governing Province, Jeju Technopark, local companies and research institutes. It opens semiconductors up not only to electronics majors but also to students from other schools in Jeju and to working engineers, and helps the people trained here go on to careers in Jeju and around the world.

Activities

What we do

EDUCATION

Practical curriculum

Four fields (device design and process, circuit design, system design, test and measurement), taught step by step from foundation to practical courses. Students carry a design through simulation, layout, fabrication and measurement.

View curriculum →

CONNECTION

Links to industry and jobs

Guest lectures and mentoring by engineers, site visits and internships, workation, short courses for working engineers, an annual showcase and hiring events.

COLLABORATION

Regional collaboration

The university runs the education, companies and research institutes bring industry needs into the courses, and the provincial government and Technopark support the operation.

Who we train

Who we train, and where they fit in the full cycle

We teach the semiconductor cycle in four fields, from design through fabrication to verification, and use campus labs together with partner facilities so that students make a real chip and a module.

① Device Design & Process (partial)Transistor design (TCAD) · device characteristics · hands-on with part of the front-end (oxidation, photo, etch, deposition)
② Circuit Design & FabricationAnalog, mixed-signal and RF circuits — simulation, layout, tape-out
③ System DesignDigital and systems — Verilog, FPGA, RISC-V, SoC
④ Test & MeasurementBack-end — EDS test, packaging; device, circuit and module measurement
