Curriculum

We do not replace the regular curriculum; we add hands-on extracurricular courses on top of it in three levels: Foundation → Practical I → Practical II & guest lectures.

STEP 1SimulationDesign with industrial EDA tools
STEP 2LayoutFrom circuit to chip drawing
STEP 3Tape-outChip fabrication
STEP 4MeasurementVerify with instruments
Field · trackFoundationPractical IPractical II & lectures
① Process & DevicesDevice characteristics · process introDevice design · fab practiceAdvanced devices & processes
② Circuits — Analog / Mixed-signalDC characteristics · tool basicsAmplifiers · Op-amp to LDOADC · DC-DC
② Circuits — RFS-parameters · tool basicsLNA · VCOTransceivers · Tape-out
③ Systems — DigitalVerilog · tool basicsFPGA · RISC-VNPU · AI accelerators · SoC

④ Test & Measurement runs as the measurement and fabrication course shared by all four tracks — from basic instruments to device I-V, circuit performance, module measurement and chip probing.

Facilities

Facilities

Teaching labs · server room

Engineering Building 3, Jeju National University. Separate design and measurement labs; a GPU server runs simulation and design tools.

Industrial EDA tools · instruments

Cadence · Siemens · Synopsys · Silvaco design tools and instruments such as oscilloscopes, in an environment that mirrors industry.

Workation space

An open space at the Jeju Knowledge Industry Center for working-engineer courses, lectures and company collaboration.
