Why this program is in Jeju, and how it proceeds

Why Jeju

  • IND
    Semiconductors already lead Jeju's exportsbut only a few companies drive them
  • EDU
    Theory is taught; hands-on design, verification and measurement are notfew courses use industrial tools and equipment
  • JOBS
    No clear path from studying semiconductors to working in Jejubuilding that path is where this program starts

Direction

  • 1
    Foundation coursesBuild the foundation and lab courses in all four fields first
  • 2
    Practical coursesExpand the advanced practical courses field by field
  • 3
    Open educationOnline theory basics, workation and partner facilities widen the door
  • 4
    Industry and jobsInternships, site visits and hiring events connect study to careers
  • 5
    A lasting organizationEstablish the Jeju Silicon Convergence Center (J-SCC) in 2028 to carry the work beyond the program
